# Deliberately difficult sample questions

Use **Use sample** in the sidebar, then try:

1. What exception changes both the normal submission timing and the normal approver, and what filing clock replaces the usual deadline?
2. A traveler cancels because of ordinary severe weather. Do they need fresh approval, and what evidence deadline applies?
3. Compare the Harbor City summit lodging exception with the urgent safety exception: what does each change?
4. If an evacuation request is filed 20 days after return, does the 30-day reimbursement rule make it timely?
5. What is the mileage reimbursement rate? *(Expected: explicit insufficient evidence.)*

These questions require exception handling, linking facts across pages, resisting a tempting but incorrect rule, or declining to answer.
